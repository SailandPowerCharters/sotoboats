A PROPER SAILING ESCAPE

For people who want to sail, not just sit on a boat.

This experience is aimed at guests who already have some sailing experience and want to spend meaningful time under sail. The exact passage plan is always subject to weather, sea conditions and the skipper’s decision.

There is time in Marina Smir between the outward and return passages, giving you two nights in Morocco before sailing back to Estepona.
